# Bundle business models and UK forced-demand drivers (as of 19 Nov 2026)

Scope: cross-cutting models for "website + marketing + automation" recurring bundles sold to SMBs, and UK regulatory/platform changes 2025-2028 that create forced demand. Research was done with WebSearch only (around 20 queries, no full-page fetches), so most figures come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. Treat the precise numbers as "verify before quoting" and check the primary source, especially where flagged.

## Q1. What UK SMBs spend on websites, marketing and software, whether they see value, and SMB churn

### Takeaway
I found no authoritative UK survey (FSB, BCC, Xero) giving a per-business monthly spend on websites or marketing. The best weak signal is that most SMEs spend under £250 a month on marketing. Satisfaction is low: only around 1 in 5 UK small businesses feel "very confident" their marketing works. SMB SaaS loses roughly 3-7% of customers a month, which implies a 1-3 year customer lifetime. A bundle priced above about £250 a month therefore has to prove its ROI clearly, ideally by tying itself to leads and bookings.

### Cited Findings
- In a 2025 survey by UK marketing consultants, 58% of SMEs spent less than £250/month on marketing. The sample size was not disclosed, and Whito says no UK survey credibly measures the average share of revenue that small firms spend on marketing. — [Whito: UK marketing budget by revenue](https://whito.co.uk/research/uk-marketing-budget-by-revenue/)
- 22% of UK small businesses feel "very confident" about their marketing effectiveness (vendor survey, Constant Contact). — [Constant Contact / Webull summary](https://www.webull.com/news/13447832745108480)
- Capterra 2025 Tech Trends (350 UK respondents out of 3,500 globally): 76% of UK organisations planned to increase software budgets in 2025. Businesses whose software purchases disappointed them were twice as likely to overspend the next year. — [Capterra UK software buying trends 2025](https://www.capterra.co.uk/blog/7557/uk-software-buying-trends-2025-research)
- Monzo: British small businesses spend £4.8bn a year on tax and accounting software. This is a sector-wide total, not a per-business figure, and is vendor-reported. — [South East Business, Jan 2026](https://southeastbusiness.com/2026/01/20/uk-small-businesses-invest-nearly-5bn-a-year-in-tax-and-accounting-software/)
- Software waste estimates: Fasthosts says SMEs could waste up to £10k a year on unused subscriptions (a hypothetical model). Marketing Signals models £7.6bn of UK waste a year, or about £6,333 per small business and £1,164 per micro business (modelled, not surveyed). — [IT Brief UK](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/uk-smes-could-waste-gbp-10-000-on-unused-software); [SME Today](https://www.smetoday.co.uk/finance/uk-smes-wasting-up-to-10k-a-year-on-unused-saas-tools/)
- SMB SaaS monthly churn benchmarks fall between 3% and 7% a month (30-58% a year). Focus Digital cites 6.4% a month. A 2026 cohort study reports SMB monthly churn rising from 4.4% (2022) to 6.2%. Another source gives a 4.7% median monthly logo churn and 31% annual SMB logo churn. These are benchmark aggregators, so definitions vary. — [Vena](https://venasolutions.com/blog/saas-churn-rate); [Visionary Marketing cohort study 2026](https://visionary-marketing.co.uk/blog/saas-churn-benchmarks-2026); [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/?p=6073); [CRV 2026](https://crv.com/content/saas-churn-rate)
- Public vertical SMB platforms retain revenue in the low 90s. Weave's net revenue retention was 94% and gross revenue retention 90% in Q3 2025, falling to NRR 93% and GRR 89% in Q4 2025. Management expects GRR to recover to 91-93%. — [Weave Q3 2025 (Quantisnow)](https://quantisnow.com/insight/weave-announces-third-quarter-2025-financial-results-6252364); [Weave Q4 2025 (Barchart)](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/313943/weave-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial-results); [Yahoo call summary](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/weave-communications-inc-q4-2025-133000767.html)
- Thryv's net revenue retention for established SaaS customers was about 94% (third-party analysis). — [Thryv FY2025 coverage (MarketChameleon)](https://marketchameleon.com/articles/b/2026/2/27/thryv-saas-growth-ai-platform-2025-results)

### Inferences
- A sub-£250/month anchor for most SMEs suggests a solo founder's bundle should sit around £150-£400/month for micro firms, with higher tiers only where a clear revenue figure (bookings or jobs won) can be shown.
- Even well-run vertical platforms lose about 10% of revenue a year gross. A solo agency-style bundle should plan for 3-5% monthly logo churn and design against it with annual contracts, setup fees, owning the client's domain or number migration, and monthly ROI reports.

### Gaps
- No FSB, BCC, Xero or Sage figure surfaced for per-business monthly website or marketing spend. FSB and Xero Small Business Insights pages were not fetched.
- No agency-specific client retention benchmark for the UK was found.
- No Visa or Barclays SMB spend data was found.

## Q2. Vertical "growth platforms" that bundle website + marketing + CRM/automation: scale, pricing, lessons, failures

### Takeaway
The winners own a daily workflow (phones and messaging for Weave and Podium, booking and payments for Vagaro, Mindbody and Pabau, practice management for Dentally) and then upsell marketing and AI on top. Companies that sold marketing as a standalone service (Yell/Hibu, Thryv's directory business) declined. AI "agents" are now the fastest-growing revenue line (Podium reports $150M in AI ARR). The lesson for a solo founder: use a sticky operational wedge (booking, phones or enquiry handling) and sell marketing as an add-on on top of it, not the other way round.

### Cited Findings
- **Thryv (FY2025):** SaaS revenue was $461.0M, up 34.2%. Excluding the Keap acquisition, it was $391.4M, up 18.6%. Marketing Services (legacy directories) revenue was $324.0M, down 32.6%. Total revenue was $785.0M versus $824.2M in 2024. SaaS adjusted EBITDA margin was 16% (+400bp). SaaS is now over 62% of revenue, and Thryv aims to be pure-play SaaS by 2028. It is repositioning as an AI-enabled "Market, Sell, Grow" platform. — [Thryv press release](https://www.thryv.com/news/thryv-achieves-saas-revenue-growth-of-34-in-full-year-2025-shifts-focus-to-ai-enabled-market-sell-grow-platform-to-empower-smbs/); [BusinessWire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260226660000/en/Thryv-Achieves-SaaS-Revenue-Growth-of-34-in-Full-Year-2025-Shifts-Focus-to-AI-Enabled-Market-Sell-Grow-Platform-to-Empower-SMBs)
- In Q2 2025, Thryv's SaaS grew 49% while EBITDA fell 36%, because the legacy decline accelerated. This shows the cost of managing a declining marketing-services base. — [Panabee](https://www.panabee.com/news/thryv-earnings-q2-2025-report)
- **Weave (dental, optometry, vets, medical):** Q3 2025 revenue was $61.3M, up 17.1%. 2026 revenue guidance was $273-276M. Retention is softening (NRR 93%, GRR 89% at Q4 2025). A Seeking Alpha analyst downgraded the stock because operating progress "remains elusive". — [BusinessWire Q3 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251030453635/en); [Barchart Q4](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/313943/weave-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial-results); [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4839584-weave-communications-grows-but-operating-progress-remains-elusive-downgrade)
- **Podium (company-reported, Oct 2026):** Podium reports more than $150M in AI-specific ARR within 2.5 years (its original software business took 8 years to reach that), and more than 20,000 AI agents deployed. Podium does not define how AI ARR is calculated, and press note it is unclear how much is new spend versus reclassified revenue. An earlier milestone was $100M in AI ARR at 300% year-on-year growth. Roughly 50% of AI revenue reportedly comes from upgrades by existing customers (secondary source). — [RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/podium-ai-agents-150m-arr); [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/podium-surpasses-150-million-in-ai-agent-annual-recurring-revenue-and-deploys-more-than-20000-ai-agents/); [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/124876-podium-passes-100m-in-ai-agent-arr-on-300-year-on-year-growth/)
- **Birdeye:** passed $100M ARR (company-announced, date not shown in results). — [Localogy](https://www.localogy.com/?p=41201)
- **Vagaro:** raised $64.5M in total (Growth Equity II, Nov 2021, led by FTV Capital). Revenue is not disclosed. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/vagaro/financials)
- **Published entry prices for booking/practice platforms** (from a vendor comparison by Pabau, so treat with caution): Vagaro about $30/month per calendar, Pabau about $62, WellnessLiving about $69, Mindbody about $99 (Mindbody does not publish rates). — [Pabau: Vagaro alternatives](https://pabau.com/blog/vagaro-alternative/); [Pabau: Mindbody alternatives](https://pabau.com/blog/mindbody-alternatives/)
- **Yell/Hibu (UK failure case):** the former Yell rebranded as Hibu. It built up about £2.3bn of debt from acquisitions, then went through a debt-for-equity restructuring in 2013 in which lenders took control, while its management talked of "one million SME customers". GlobalCapital headlines record digital revenue declining after the rebrand. This is older data (2012-2013); I found no 2025-26 reporting. — [VCPost 2013](https://www.vcpost.com/articles/12077/20130725/uk-yellow-pages-operator-hibu-lenders-agree-restructuring-deal.htm); [GlobalCapital](https://www.globalcapital.com/article/28mss7ctnpdchq9vh4r9d/securitization/clos/rebranded-yell-to-restructure-as-even-digital-declines); [MarketScreener](https://marketscreener.com/quote/stock/HIBU-PLC-4004156/news/Hibu-PLC-Hibu-reaches-deal-for-creditors-to-take-control-of-company-17125523)

### Inferences
- Thryv's numbers are the clearest evidence that "marketing services sold to SMBs" shrinks (-33% a year) while "software the SMB runs on" grows. A solo founder should make the bundle feel like an operating system (inbox, bookings, follow-ups) rather than a marketing retainer.
- Podium's figures, even discounted for being company-reported, suggest AI enquiry handling and "AI employee" features are what SMBs are currently willing to pay extra for.
- The common pattern is to start in one vertical with one workflow, then expand: Weave went from dental to optometry and vets, and Thryv bought Keap. Weave's falling retention, partly attributed to newer vertical cohorts, also shows that expanding into new verticals can hurt retention.

### Gaps
- No verified ARR or customer counts were found for Pabau, Dentally (Henry Schein One), Bookinglab, Mindbody/Booker, ServiceTitan Marketing Pro or Birdeye (2025-26).
- No Podium layoff or valuation update was found in this pass.
- No current (2025-26) data on Yell's state was found.

## Q3. The productised service to SaaS path, and GoHighLevel white-label economics and saturation

### Takeaway
GoHighLevel's $497/month "SaaS Pro" plan lets an agency resell a white-labelled CRM, website and automation stack to unlimited clients. The economics look strong on paper (about $97-$297/month per client against a fixed $497), but generic GHL agencies are widely described as oversaturated, and I found no GHL-specific churn data. Positioning in a single niche is the consensus way to stand out, and it fits the founder's plan. GHL is a reasonable way to prototype the service before building proprietary vertical SaaS.

### Cited Findings
- SaaS Mode is only available on the $497/month Agency Pro / SaaS Pro tier. Annual billing brings the effective cost to about $398-$414/month, and HighLevel changes its plans and prices frequently. — [Webdew: GHL SaaS mode](https://www.webdew.com/blog/gohighlevel-saas-mode); [Brandblast, Sep 2026](https://www.brandblast.com/blog/gohighlevel-saas-mode-pricing); [Ecosire pricing 2026](https://ecosire.com/blog/gohighlevel-pricing-worth-it-2026)
- A GHL agency (an interested party) concludes that generic "GoHighLevel agency" positioning is oversaturated while niche-specific positioning is not, citing common Reddit questions about whether a GHL SaaS is still worth starting. — [GHL Scale Up](https://www.ghlscaleup.com/services/gohighlevel-saas-mode)
- No GHL-specific churn data was found. Guides use general SMB SaaS benchmarks and advise keeping churn below 5% a month. An aggregator's AI-analysed Reddit sentiment on GHL pricing was 38% positive, 22% neutral and 40% negative (methodology unclear). — [Ecosire white-label guide](https://ecosire.com/blog/ghl-white-label-saas-guide); [SyncGTM review 2026](https://syncgtm.com/blog/gohighlevel-review-2026)
- A practitioner review titled "the brutal truth after 5 years of real agency use" exists (critical perspective). — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/robert_denim/gohighlevel-review-2026-the-brutal-truth-after-5-years-of-real-agency-use-n1m)

### Inferences
- A solo founder could launch the bundle on GHL (or a similar platform) for about £400/month fixed cost. At 10 clients paying £200-£300/month, gross margin before the founder's time would be above 80%. The constraints are platform dependency, commoditisation (thousands of GHL resellers offer the same snapshot) and no proprietary IP.
- The productised-service-to-SaaS path: (1) run the service manually or on GHL for 10-30 clients in one niche; (2) document the one workflow that drives retention, such as AI enquiry triage to booking; (3) rebuild that workflow as a standalone product with niche integrations (practice management systems, trade job software) that GHL does not offer. Podium and Weave followed a similar wedge-then-expand pattern.

### Gaps
- No primary Reddit thread data or measured GHL reseller churn was collected. All GHL sources are vendor or affiliate content.
- No documented UK case study of productised service to SaaS was found in this pass.

## Q4. Which bundle components SMBs value most versus commodity, and margins of AI-native services

### Takeaway
The strongest evidence of value is for components that capture revenue, above all answering calls and enquiries. UK trade surveys show a third of firms lose work to missed calls, and 69% of callers will not leave a voicemail. Websites and basic SEO are commodities, and their value is falling as Google AI Overviews cut organic clicks by 34-61%. Reviews and Google Business Profile matter more as AI search increasingly decides which local firms appear.

### Cited Findings
- Fix Radio survey of more than 220 UK tradespeople: 34% had lost work by not answering the phone, and 60% regularly struggle to take calls on site. — [Trade2Base summary](https://www.trade2base.com/blog/trade-business-call-handling-uk)
- Everreach survey (more than 250 trade small businesses, 2024): plumbers potentially lose about £500 a week (about £26k a year) to missed calls, and builders up to £1,000 a week. — [HVP Magazine](https://www.hvpmag.co.uk/Missed-calls-cost-plumbers-over-amppound25000-a-year-according-to-survey)
- Moneypenny (300 micro businesses, a vendor): 33% let calls go to voicemail or ring out, and 69% of callers don't leave a voicemail. — [Moneypenny small business call report](https://www.moneypenny.com/uk/resources/news/small-business-call-report/)
- BT Business: missed calls cost UK businesses £3.7bn a year, and 67% of SMEs miss at least one crucial call a day. A Softomate figure of £30bn conflicts with this and is weakly sourced. — [Irish News](https://www.irishnews.com/news/business/missed-phone-calls-have-cost-local-businesses-nearly-3000-this-year-3MJCU4WMCNG45HK4R2RDUIMLQI/); [Softomate](https://www.softomatesolutions.com/blog/missed-calls-cost-uk-small-business/)
- Ahrefs (300k keywords, Dec 2025 data): AI Overviews cut position-1 organic click-through rate by 58%. Seer Interactive found a 61% drop. SparkToro/Similarweb found 68% of US Google searches ended without a click in early 2026. A claimed 34% fall in clicks to local business websites (Search Engine Land) could not be verified. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update); [Digitaleer](https://www.digitaleer.com/ai-impact-google-search-traffic-2026/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-overviews-cut-organic-clicks-38-field-study-finds/)
- WhatsApp Business Platform moved from per-conversation to per-message pricing on 1 July 2025. Each marketing template is now billed individually, utility templates sent inside an open customer-service window are free, and Meta can change prices only on the first day of a quarter. — [Twilio notice](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/30304057900699-Notice-Changes-to-WhatsApp-s-Pricing-July-2025); [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- Podium's reported AI ARR growth (see Q2) is market evidence that SMBs pay extra for AI agents that handle enquiries and leads. — [RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/podium-ai-agents-150m-arr)

### Inferences
- Value hierarchy (from evidence plus inference): (1) capturing missed calls and enquiries with AI and booking them in; (2) automated review requests and Google Business Profile management; (3) follow-ups and reactivation via SMS or WhatsApp; (4) admin automation; (5) website and SEO, which are now commodity "table stakes". Price the bundle on 1-3 and include 5.
- Margin structure (inference, no source found): the marginal cost of AI-native services is mostly LLM and voice minutes, telephony and messaging fees, for perhaps a few £ to tens of £ per client per month, plus platform fees. Gross margins of 70-85% look plausible, but voice minutes and WhatsApp per-message fees scale with usage, so the bundle needs usage caps or pass-through pricing.

### Gaps
- No independent study of AI-native agency gross margins was found.
- No UK survey ranking SMB willingness to pay by component was found.
- I did not research iMessage/RCS business messaging or changes to Google Business Profile features (e.g. Q&A, chat removal).

## Q5. UK forced-demand drivers 2025-2028: what, who, when, how many, which service

### Takeaway
The largest and most certain driver is Making Tax Digital for Income Tax: about 864,000 sole traders and landlords from April 2026, with thresholds dropping to £30k in 2027 and £20k in 2028. The UK B2B e-invoicing mandate (April 2029) follows. The landlord stack (Renters' Rights Act, the private rented sector database and ombudsman, EPC C by 2030, Awaab's Law extension consultation) and employment law (Employment Rights Act 2025, with SSP from day one in April 2026 and a six-month unfair dismissal qualifying period from January 2027) create admin-automation demand. Several drivers have slipped: Companies House software-only accounts filing has moved from April 2027 to around 2028 or later, and identity verification for filers is now no earlier than November 2027.

### Cited Findings

**Tax and filing**
- **MTD for Income Tax.** Who: sole traders and landlords. When: April 2026 for qualifying income over £50k, April 2027 over £30k, April 2028 over £20k. How many: about 864,000 in the first wave (widely quoted, probably from HMRC; the original source was not confirmed). About 100,000 had joined voluntarily. Need: quarterly digital records and submissions through compatible software, plus bookkeeping help. — [GOV.UK policy paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/extension-of-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-to-sole-traders-and-landlords/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-for-sole-traders-and-landlords); [Bytestart](https://www.bytestart.co.uk/news-insights/864000-sole-traders-and-landlords-face-new-mtd-reporting-rules-from-april-2026/); [BRC](https://brc.org.uk/news-and-events/news/finance/2025/ungated/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax/)
- **UK e-invoicing mandate.** Budget 2025 confirmed that all VAT invoices for B2B (and possibly B2G) must be structured e-invoices from 1 April 2029. It is subject to Parliament; PDFs will not count; the technical format is expected around Budget 2026. Sources conflict on phasing and on whether Peppol is confirmed. Who: all VAT-registered businesses (VAT registration threshold is £90k, not verified this pass). — [Comarch](https://erp.comarch.com/trade-and-services/data-management/legal-regulation-changes/uk-budget-2025-confirms-mandatory-b2b-e-invoicing-for-2029); [MHA](https://www.mha.co.uk/insights/mandatory-e-invoicing-from-2029-why-businesses-should-start-preparing-now); [Sovos](https://sovos.com/regulatory-updates/vat/united-kingdom-e-invoicing-expected-in-april-2029/); [ACCA](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/technical-activities/technical-resources-search/2025/November/Promoting-electronic-invoicing-UK-businesses-public-sector.html)
- **Companies House (ECCTA).** Existing directors, PSCs and LLP members had to verify their identity by about 18 November 2026 (the end of the 12-month transition). Only about 55% of directors, 50% of LLP members and 42% of PSCs had verified by August 2026. Mandatory verification for presenters (filers) and the ACSP requirement for agents have been delayed to no earlier than November 2027. Software-only accounts filing and mandatory profit and loss filing for small and micro companies will not start in April 2027; reports suggest around April 2028, with at least 21 months' notice. — [HSF Kramer](https://www.hsfkramer.com/notes/corporate/2026-posts/confirmation-of-delays-in-idv-rollout); [DWF, Aug 2026](https://dwfgroup.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/8/delays-to-implementation-of-compulsory-identity-verification); [LearnSignal](https://www.learnsignal.com/blog/companies-house-identity-verification-guide-2026/); [TaxAssist](https://www.taxassist.co.uk/ayr/resources/articles/changes-at-companies-house-what-this-means-for-you)

**Property / landlords**
- **Renters' Rights Act 2025.** Phase 1 took effect on 1 May 2026, abolishing section 21 and bringing in most tenancy reforms. Phase 2 is the private rented sector landlord database (from late 2026) followed by the landlord ombudsman (possibly not open until 2028). Phase 3, the Decent Homes Standard for private rentals, is expected around 2035-2037 (to be confirmed). Need: tenancy and compliance admin, document management, communication logs, and handling of repairs. — [GOV.UK roadmap PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6915beb8bc34c86ce4e6e730/Implementing_Renters_Rights_Act_2025_-_roadmap.pdf); [NRLA](https://www.nrla.org.uk/news/renters-rights-act-2025-key-dates-for-landlords); [Ward Hadaway](https://www.wardhadaway.com/insights/updates/renters-rights-act-implementation-dates/)
- **Awaab's Law.** Phase 2 for social housing starts on 30 November 2026. Extending it to private tenancies is under consultation (9 Oct to 18 Dec 2026), with no date set; 2027 is the earliest likely. Need: tracking response times for damp and mould reports, tenant communications and evidence trails. — [Mortgage Solutions, Oct 2026](https://www.mortgagesolutions.co.uk/mortgage-news/2026/10/09/consultation-to-extend-awaabs-law-to-prs-announced/); [Trowers](https://www.trowers.com/insights/awaabs-law-in-the-private-rented-sector-the-governments-consultation); [Osbornes](https://osborneslaw.com/blog/awaabs-law-private-landlords/)
- **MEES, domestic private rentals.** The government response (21 Jan 2026) set 1 October 2030 as the date for EPC C equivalence in England and Wales, with a £10k cost cap (down from a proposed £15k). Short-term lets are excluded. About 2.5-2.9 million private rented homes are below C. Still subject to Parliament. Need: EPC assessments, retrofit project management, and the trades and installers involved. — [GOV.UK consultation outcome](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/improving-the-energy-performance-of-privately-rented-homes-2025-update); [Elmhurst Energy](https://www.elmhurstenergy.co.uk/blog/2026/01/22/domestic-private-rented-properties-required-to-meet-epc-band-c-by-october-2030/); [NRLA](https://www.nrla.org.uk/news/epc-c-date-pushed-back-but-what-does-this-mean-for-you)

**Employment**
- **Employment Rights Act 2025 (revised timetable published Feb 2026).** April 2026: SSP from day one with the lower earnings limit removed, day-one paternity and unpaid parental leave, and the Fair Work Agency launched. January 2027: the unfair dismissal qualifying period falls from 2 years to 6 months (applying to existing staff), the compensation cap is removed, and fire-and-rehire is restricted. Sources disagree on when tribunal time limits extend to 6 months (April vs October 2026). Who: every employer. Need: HR documentation, probation and onboarding workflows, absence tracking, contracts. — [DLA Piper revised timeline](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/revised-timeline-for-implementation-of-the-employment-rights-act-2025); [Citation](https://www.citation.co.uk/employment-rights-act-2025-implementation-timeline/); [Slaughter and May](https://www.slaughterandmay.com/horizon-scanning/2026/governance-and-sustainability/implementing-the-employment-rights-act-2025/)

**Premises and security**
- **Martyn's Law (Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025).** Royal Assent was on 3 April 2025, with at least 24 months to implementation, so enforcement is expected no earlier than spring 2027. The standard tier covers premises with 200-799 people, counting staff as well as the public; the enhanced tier is 800+. Standard-tier premises must notify the SIA and have procedures for evacuation, invacuation/lockdown and communication, plus staff awareness. Who: pubs, venues, gyms, places of worship, larger shops and similar. The number of premises was not found. Need: procedure documents, staff training records, SIA notification. — [Travers Smith](https://www.traverssmith.com/knowledge/knowledge-container/martyns-law-prepare-now-for-implementation-in-spring-2027/); [Keoghs](https://keoghs.co.uk/keoghs-insight/martyns-law-anticipated-guidance-and-implementation-timeline); [ProtectUK](https://www.protectuk.police.uk/standard-tier)

**Waste**
- **Simpler Recycling (England).** Businesses with more than 10 FTE had to comply from 31 March 2025. Micro firms (fewer than 10 FTE, counted across the whole enterprise) must comply by 31 March 2027, including flexible plastics, and Environment Agency civil sanctions start then. Many micro firms are reported to be unprepared (Hills Waste research). Need: separate waste collection contracts, a fairly low-value service. — [Rushcliffe BC](https://www.rushcliffe.gov.uk/business/simpler-recycling-for-businesses/); [Envirotec, Jul 2026](https://envirotecmagazine.com/2026/07/23/are-firms-ready-for-simpler-recycling/); [Everflow](https://everflowutilities.com/insights/simpler-recycling-four-things-micro-businesses-need-to-know)

**Data and marketing compliance**
- **Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.** Most provisions came into force on 5 February 2026. Maximum PECR fines (covering cookies, marketing email and SMS) rose from £500k to UK GDPR levels (£17.5M or 4% of turnover), and the "seriousness" threshold was removed. Consent is no longer needed for analytics-only cookies, but advertising cookies still require it. The ICO's final storage and access guidance came out in late April 2026. The complaints procedure requirement starts on 19 June 2026. Need: cookie banners and consent setup, marketing-consent capture in CRMs, complaints workflows. ### Inferences: ranking for a small AI service business
Ranked by size of opportunity times fit with a websites + marketing + automation bundle (my judgement):
1. **MTD for Income Tax (2026-2028).** Very large and certain, with recurring quarterly pain. It mostly benefits accountants and bookkeeping software, but bundling "MTD-ready receipt capture, bookkeeping and invoicing" into a trades or landlord bundle (or partnering with an accountant) is a strong hook. The e-invoicing mandate (2029) extends it.
2. **Landlord and letting-agent stack** (Renters' Rights Act phases, database and ombudsman, Awaab's Law extension, EPC C by 2030). Hundreds of thousands of small landlords and agents need communication logs, repair SLAs and document trails, which is a strong vertical SaaS candidate. It also creates downstream demand for trades (damp and mould, retrofit, EPC assessors), who need lead handling.
3. **Employment Rights Act** (April 2026 and January 2027). Applies to every employer; the 6-month unfair dismissal rule makes probation and documentation workflows essential. Good as an admin-automation add-on.
4. **AI search / Google click loss.** Not regulation, but the forced demand that bears most directly on the bundle's marketing half: reviews, Google Business Profile and AI visibility replace "SEO websites".
5. **DUAA / PECR fine exposure.** Cheap to bundle (consent, cookies, complaints) and a useful compliance upsell for websites and CRMs.
6. **Martyn's Law (from spring 2027).** A niche but real need for hospitality, venues and gyms; procedure documents and training logs are productisable.
7. **Companies House IDV and filing changes.** Mostly delayed or already handled by accountants and the GOV.UK One Login; a weak opportunity for a bundle founder.
8. **Simpler Recycling (micro firms, March 2027).** A real deadline but the need is a waste contract, which fits poorly with a digital bundle.
